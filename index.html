Questions

Before you start a group.

Do my friends need to pay?

No. Only the organizer subscribes to Pro, and everyone in that organizer’s group gets it. Friends can always join free groups.

Do my friends need an iPhone?

Yes. Ready4Golf is an iPhone app (iOS 17 or later) and there is no Android version yet. Everyone in the group needs an iPhone and to be signed in to iCloud.

Does it book the tee time?

No. It finds when enough of your group can play. Then you call the course or book online. A button on the plan opens GolfNow to help you search.

What happens if someone doesn’t answer?

They show as “waiting on” and are not counted out, so a quiet friend never kills the plan by accident.

What does it do with my data?

Your group lives in your own iCloud, shared only with the people you invite. There is no Ready4Golf account or ad tracking. See the privacy policy.

How many people can be in a group?

Free groups hold up to 4 players and you can organize 1 group. Pro removes both limits.
